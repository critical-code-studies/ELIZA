# SLIP (ELIZA)

- `SLIP-1965.mad`: the SLIP listing found among Joseph Weizenbaum's papers at MIT (dome.mit.edu,
  handle 1721.3/201707): 67 routines, each compiled on its own (on cards, a deck), 51 in MAD and 16 in FAP. Transcribed by Arthur Schwarz;
  taken from elizagen.org (github.com/jeffshrager/elizagen.org,
  1965_Weizenbaum_MAD-SLIP/Slip/CTSS/02-000311065.mad) on 12 October 2026.
- `bcdit.mad`, `inlstl.mad`, `letter.mad`: routines ELIZA needs that the listing lacks,
  reconstructed in 2024 by Anthony Hay, Arthur Schwarz and Rupert Lane for the restoration of
  ELIZA on CTSS; from the same folder (SLIP-reconstructed/).

The licence: `LICENSE.txt`. The research bench reads these files as the layer SLIP, under ELIZA.
