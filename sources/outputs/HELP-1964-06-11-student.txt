                    Computer Output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP

STUDENT:  (I AM HAVING TROUBLE WRITING PROGRAMS)

PROF:     (IS IT BECAUSE YOU ARE HAVING TROUBLE WRITING PROGRAMS THAT
          YOU ARE COMING TO ME)

STUDENT:  (YES)

PROF:     (I SEE)

STUDENT:  (MY PROGRAMS CONFUSE THE WORDS YOU AND ME)

PROF:     (WHY DO YOU THINK YOUR PROGRAMS CONFUSE THE WORDS I AND YOU)

STUDENT:  (I DONT KNOW - YOU SEE WHAT I MEAN THOUGH)

PROF:     (DO YOU WANT TO KNOW ME)

STUDENT:  (WELL, THAT ISNT REALLY WHAT I'M HERE FOR)

PROF:     (THAT IS INTERESTING)

STUDENT:  (APART FROM THAT, CAN YOU HELP ME)

PROF:     (YOU BELIEVE I CAN HELP YOU - DONT YOU)

STUDENT:  (I TRUST YOU CAN HELP - YOU'VE BEEN HIGHLY RECOMMENDED)

PROF:     (CLEARLY YOU WANT TO TRUST ME)

STUDENT:  (YOU SOUND MORE LIKE A PSYCHIATRIST THAN A PROGRAMMER)

PROF:     (YOU THINK I SOUND MORE LIKE A PSYCHIATRIST THAN A PROGRAMMER)

STUDENT:  (EITHER THAT, OR YOU DONT KNOW MUCH ABOUT COMPUTERS)

PROF:     (YOU THINK I DONT KNOW MUCH ABOUT COMPUTERS)

STUDENT:  (THAT SUSPICION IS BEGINNING TO COME TO MY MIND)

PROF:     (REALLY - YOUR MIND)
; [R of REALLY struck over in black]

STUDENT:  (WHY DO YOU INSULT ME)

PROF:     (YOU IMAGINE THAT I INSULT YOU)

STUDENT:  (WELL THAT CRACK SET ME OFF A LITTLE)

PROF:     (HMMM)

STUDENT:  (YOU ARE A PROGRAMMER)

PROF:     (WHAT MAKES YOU THINK I AM A PROGRAMMER)

STUDENT:  (YOU'RE RIGHT, THERE ISN'T MUCH EVIDENCE FOR IT - IS THERE)
; [page 3]

                                                                  Page 2

                 Computer Output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP (Cont'd)

PROF:     (LETS EXPLORE FURTHER WHY YOUR PROGRAMS CONFUSE THE WORDS I AND YOU)

STUDENT:  (PERHAPS ITS BECAUSE THEY HAVE SIMILAR BIT PATTERNS OR SOMETHING)

PROF:     (IS THAT THE REAL REASON)

STUDENT:  (I DOUBT IT)

PROF:     (YOU SAY YOU DOUBT IT)

STUDENT:  (WELL, AS YOU KNOW, COMPUTERS DONT WORK LIKE THAT)

PROF:     (YOU THINK I KNOW COMPUTERS DONT WORK LIKE THAT)

STUDNET:  (NO - I FINALLY DECIDED YOU DONT KNOW ANYTHING ABOUT HOW COMPUTERS WORK)

PROF:     (OH)

STUDENT:  (I'M CONVINCED YOU'RE QUITE SICK, YOU NEED HELP)

PROF:     (YOU THINK I NEED HELP)

STUDENT:  (YES INDEED - I KNOW OF A COMPUTER PROGRAM THAT CAN CURE YOU)

PROF:     (I SEE)

STUDENT:  (YOU SHOULD GO TO SEE THE PEOPLE AT PROJECT MAC)

PROF:     (YOU THINK I SHOULD GO TO SEE THE PEOPLE AT PROJECT MAC)

STUDENT:  (BUT DONT TRY TO TELL THEM YOU ARE A COMPUTER EXPERT)

PROF:     (WHAT MAKES YOU THINK I AM A COMPUTER EXPERT)

STUDENT:  (NOTHING - ABSOLUTELY NOTHING)

PROF:     (HMMM)

STUDENT:  (YOU ARE DISAPPOINTED)

PROF:     (WHAT MAKES YOU THINK I AM DISAPPOINTED)

STUDENT:  (THE WAY YOU PLEAD WITH ME)

PROF:     (PERHAPS YOU PLEAD WITH ME)

STUDENT:  (I CANT FOLLOW YOUR REASONING)
; [S of REASONING struck over in black]

PROF:     (HAVE YOU TRIED)

STUDENT:  (I HAVE)

PROF:     (YOU SAY YOU HAVE)
; [page 4]

                                                                  Page 3

                 Computer Output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP (Cont'd)

STUDENT:  (YES - BUT IT DOESN'T HELP MUCH)

PROF:     (I SEE)

STUDENT:  (WHY DONT YOU COME WITH ME TO SEE THAT LOVELY PROGRAM AT PROJECT MAC)

PROF:     (YOU WISH ME TO COME WITH YOU)

STUDENT:  (YES - I'M SURE WE WILL BOTH FEEL BETTER AFTER YOU'VE HAD SOME REAL THERAPY)

PROF:     (AHA)

STUDENT:  (THERE - YOU FEEL BETTER ALREADY - COME ON NOW)
