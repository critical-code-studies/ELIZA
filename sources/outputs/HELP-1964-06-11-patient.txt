                    Computer output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP

PATIENT:  (I AM VERY DEPRESSED)

DOCTOR:   (HOW LONG HAVE YOU BEEN VERY DEPRESSED)

PATIENT:  (EVER SINCE MY DAD STARTED DRINKING)

DOCTOR:   (REALLY - YOUR DAD STARTED DRINKING)

PATIENT:  (YES)

DOCTOR:   (I UNDERSTAND)

PATIENT:  (HOW CAN YOU UNDERSTAND)

DOCTOR:   (YOU BELIEVE I CAN UNDERSTAND - DONT YOU)

PATIENT:  (WHY SHOULD I BELIEVE IN ANYONE)

DOCTOR:  (PERHAPS THATS WHAT WE ARE HERE TO FIND OUT)

PATIENT:  (MAYBE YOU ARE AN ALCOHOLIC)

DOCTOR:   (WHAT MAKES YOU THINK I AM AN ALCOHOLIC)

PATIENT:  (YOU SEEM A LOT LIKE MY FATHER)

DOCTOR:   (YOU THINK I SEEM A LOT LIKE YOUR FATHER)

PATIENT:  (WELL, NO - NOT REALLY)

DOCTOR:   (WHY DO YOU SAY THAT)

PATIENT:  (FOR ONE THING - YOU DONT YELL AT ME)

DOCTOR:   (PERHAPS YOU DONT YELL AT ME)
; [DOCTOR: the C is damaged or struck over, reading like DOQTOR; ME) struck over in black]

PATIENT:  (I DONT YELL AT YOU - OR ANYONE, FOR THAT MATTER)

DOCTOR:   (DO YOU WANT TO YELL AT ME)
; [this line is retyped in black over an earlier purple line; the overstrike reads "(DO YOU WANT TO YELL AT ME)"
;  as confirmed by the ditto copy; the underlying earlier text is unreadable, its visible
;  tail running on past the corrected line as "[?][?]U)"]

PATIENT:  (I CANT GET ANGRY WITH ANYONE)

DOCTOR:   (HAVE YOU TRIED)

PATIENT:  (ONLY SECRETLY - IN MY DREAMS)

DOCTOR:   (REALLY - YOUR DREAMS)

PATIENT:  (I DREAM THE WILDEST DREAMS)
; [page 3]

                                                                  Page 2

                 Computer output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP (cont'd)

DOCTOR:   (YOU SAY YOU DREAM THE WILDEST DREAMS)

PATIENT:  (I FEEL VERY POWERFUL - IN DREAMS, THAT IS)

DOCTOR:   (TELL ME MORE ABOUT SUCH FEELINGS)

PATIENT:  (THEY FRIGHTEN ME - I WAKE UP SCREAMING)

DOCTOR:  (YOU SAY YOU WAKE UP SCREAMING)

PATIENT:  (MY WHOLE BODY SHAKES)

DOCTOR:   (REALLY - YOUR WHOLE BODY SHAKES)
; [the hyphen after REALLY is underscored, or struck over an underscore]

PATIENT:  (YES - ITS ALWAYS A TERRIBLE EXPERIENCE)

DOCTOR:   (AHA)

PATIENT:  (WHY DO YOU MAKE FUN OF ME)

DOCTOR:   (YOU IMAGINE THAT I MAKE FUN OF YOU)

PATIENT:  (NO - I KNOW YOU WERE LAUGHING AT ME)

DOCTOR:   (OH)

PATIENT:  (WHY DONT YOU PRESCRIBE SOMETHING FOR ME SO I CAN RELAX)

DOCTOR:   (YOU WISH ME TO PRESCRIBE SOMETHING FOR YOU)

PATIENT:  (ISNT THAT WHAT A DOCTOR IS SUPPOSED TO DO)

DOCTOR:   (LETS EXPLORE FURTHER WHY YOUR DAD STARTED DRINKING)

PATIENT:  (HE STARTED JUST AFTER MY MOTHER LEFT HOME)

DOCTOR:   (REALLY - YOUR MOTHER LEFT HOME)

PATIENT:  (BECAUSE OF THE DRINKING AND ALL, I GUESS)

DOCTOR:   (LETS EXPLORE FURTHER WHY YOUR MOTHER LEFT HOME)

PATIENT:  (ACTUALLY - DAD HAD STARTED TO RUN AROUND)

DOCTOR:   (HMMM)
; [H of HMMM struck over in black]

PATIENT:  (SINCE THEN, I CANT GET MY FEELINGS ABOUT HIM STRAIGHT)

DOCTOR:   (PERHAPS YOU COULD GET YOUR FEELINGS ABOUT HIM STRAIGHT IF YOU SAW
          YOUR PROBLEMS MORE REALISTICALLY)

PATIENT:  (CAN YOU HELP ME)

DOCTOR:   (YOU BELIEVE I CAN HELP YOU - DONT YOU)
; [page 4]

                                                                  Page 3

                 Computer output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP (cont'd)

PATIENT:  (YOU SAID YOU UNDERSTOOD ME AND MY DEPRESSION)

DOCTOR:   (IS THAT WHAT YOU HEARD ME SAY)

PATIENT:  (I'M NOT SURE NOW - YOU ARE ANGRY WITH ME)

DOCTOR:   (WHY DO YOU THINK I AM ANGRY WITH YOU)

PATIENT:  (YOU ACCUSED ME OF NOT LISTENING)

DOCTOR:   (YOU THINK I ACCUSED YOU OF NOT LISTENING)
; [DOCTOR: the T is struck over in black]

PATIENT:  (YES - AND IT UPSETS ME)

DOCTOR:   (I SEE)

PATIENT:  (YOU REMIND ME OF MY FATHER)

DOCTOR:   (YOU THINK I REMIND YOU OF YOUR FATHER)

PATIENT: (YOU'RE YELLING AT ME JUST AS HE DID)

DOCTOR:   (THAT IS INTERESTING)

PATIENT:  (I DONT KNOW HOW TO EXPRESS MY FEELINGS TO YOU)

DOCTOR:   (DO YOU WANT TO KNOW HOW TO EXPRESS YOUR FEELINGS TO ME)

PATIENT:  (YES - ITS ANGER - I AM FINALLY ANGRY)

DOCTOR:   (I UNDERSTAND)

PATIENT:  (YOU'RE A WONDERFUL DOCTOR - YOU CURED ME)

DOCTOR:   (PERHAPS YOU CURED ME)

PATIENT:  (I'M NOT ANGRY ANYMORE - BACK TO THE COMPUTER FACTORY FOR ME)

DOCTOR:   (WHAT ELSE DOES THAT SUGGEST TO YOU)

PATIENT:  (IT FEELS GOOD NOT TO BE ANGRY AND ALSO BE CURED)

DOCTOR:   (THAT IS INTERESTING)

PATIENT:  (NOW THAT I UNDERSTAND YOU AND DAD, I GUESS ITS TIME FOR A DRINK)

DOCTOR:   (CLEARLY YOU WANT TO UNDERSTAND ME)
; [C of CLEARLY struck over in black]

PATIENT:  (AND I DO - BELIEVE ME - SO LONG NOW)
