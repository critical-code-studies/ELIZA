# The outputs

Conversations ELIZA produced, named by script and date (the research bench's version and
reference in brackets):

- `SPACKS-1968-conversation.txt`: SPACKS conversation, 1968 [SPACKS-C68]. As printed in Edwin F.
  Taylor, "Automated Tutoring and Its Discontents", American Journal of Physics 36(6), 1968,
  496-504; transcribed by Jeff Shrager for elizagen.org (github.com/jeffshrager/elizagen.org,
  elliza/spacks.lisp); taken from there on 12 October 2026.
- `DOCTOR-1965-terrible-news.txt`: DOCTOR conversation, 1965 (terrible news) [DOC-C65-NEWS].
  Weizenbaum's papers at MIT (dome.mit.edu, handle 1721.3/201700): 32 copies of one typed page,
  undated, in the folder "Computer conversations, 1965". Read by machine (macOS Vision) across
  all copies, the majority reading taken for each line, checked against page 2; 12 October 2026.
- `HELP-1964-06-11-student.txt` [HELP-C640611-S] and `HELP-1964-06-11-patient.txt`
  [HELP-C640611-P]: typed "Computer output - June 11, 1964 - Program HELP" (handles 1721.3/201704
  and 201705), typescripts with ditto copies.
- `CONVERSATION-1964-10-01-very-depressed.txt` [C641001]: a CTSS session of 1 October 1964
  (resume oplsep), the printout edited by hand, and three typed copies (handle 1721.3/201701).
- `DOCTOR-1965-03-05-conversation.txt` [DOC-C650305]: a printout edited by hand and two typed
  copies, dated in pencil March 5, 1965 (handle 1721.3/201702).
- `DOCTOR-1965-03-22-conversation-men.txt` [DOC-C650322]: "Men are all alike", in four versions
  (handle 1721.3/201703).

These five were transcribed from the scans on 12 October 2026 and are unchecked; uncertain
readings are marked [?] or [word?]. Where the script is not named on the page, DOCTOR is inferred.
